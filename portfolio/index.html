About

Independent multidisciplinary designer and art director working across brand identity, graphic design, UI/UX and web. My focus today is increasingly on creative direction: defining the idea, visual language and system behind a project, then guiding it from concept to final execution.

I lead the creative process, make key visual decisions, structure the work and maintain consistency across every touchpoint. Where appropriate, I delegate production and technical execution to junior designers while retaining art direction, review and quality control. This allows me to focus on concept, direction, decision-making and the overall coherence of the project.
